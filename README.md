# Schumacher × Ferrari — The Golden Years

> An interactive data-driven scrollytelling about Michael Schumacher's five consecutive Formula 1 World Championships with Ferrari (2000–2004).

## About the Project

**Schumacher × Ferrari — The Golden Years** is an interactive data visualization project that explores one of the most dominant periods in Formula 1 history: Michael Schumacher's five consecutive Drivers' Championships with Ferrari between 2000 and 2004.

The project was developed as a final assignment for the **Data Visualization** course at **Universidad Torcuato Di Tella**.

Rather than presenting Schumacher's achievements as a collection of statistics, the project uses **scrollytelling** to build a visual narrative around his dominance. As the user scrolls through the story, race results, championships, statistics, photographs, videos, quotations, and interactive visualizations progressively reveal the scale of Ferrari's success during these five seasons.

## The Story

Five consecutive championships are difficult to put into perspective.

Between 2000 and 2004, Schumacher and Ferrari established an unprecedented level of dominance. Across these five seasons, Schumacher accumulated:

* **5 consecutive World Championships**
* **48 race victories**
* **616 championship points**

The goal of the project was to give these numbers context and show **how dominant this period actually was**, rather than simply stating that Schumacher won five titles.

The narrative follows each championship season individually before stepping back to look at the bigger picture: Schumacher's race results, victories, consistency, Ferrari's dominance, and the geographical distribution of his wins.

## Data Visualization

The project combines several visualization techniques to tell the story:

* **Bar charts** for comparing performance and statistics
* **Line charts** for showing changes and progression over time
* **Percentage-based visualizations** to highlight proportions
* **Chart races** to visualize the evolution of championship standings
* **Maps** to show the geographical distribution of victories
* **Animated visualizations** integrated into the scrolling narrative

These visualizations are combined with multimedia elements and explanatory text so that the data becomes part of a continuous story rather than a collection of independent charts.

## Interactive Scrollytelling

The project is structured as a vertical narrative.

As the reader scrolls through the page, different sections introduce a new season, statistic, visualization, or piece of historical context. This allows the visualizations to appear at the moment they become relevant to the story.

The experience combines:

**Narrative → Data → Visualization → Context → Interpretation**

The intention was to make the project accessible even to readers with little knowledge of Formula 1 while still providing enough data to demonstrate the scale of Schumacher and Ferrari's dominance.

## Data

The project primarily uses publicly available Formula 1 data, with **Wikipedia** serving as the main source.

The dataset focuses on relatively straightforward information such as:

* Race results
* Victories
* Podiums
* Championship standings
* Drivers and teams
* Race locations

Because the underlying data was relatively simple and structured, extensive data cleaning or preprocessing was not required.
